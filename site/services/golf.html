Golf Rounds

Cabo San Lucas Country Club

One of the most challenging and stunning courses on the peninsula.

Ask on WhatsApp

The Roy and Matt Dye-designed course sits where the Sea of Cortez meets the Pacific and the untouched desert descends into emerald water, with views of Land’s End and San Lucas Bay. Formerly the Raven Golf Club, the 18-hole championship course has gently sloping fairways, more than 80 bunkers and, at 610 yards, one of the longest holes in Mexico. Reserve your tee time today.
