Activities & Tours

Land’s End and beyond

You can't leave Cabo without visiting the famous Land's End Arch!

Ask on WhatsApp

There are lots of things to do in Cabo: razors, ATVs, camel rides, horseback riding and bicycling, in a mix of mountain, ocean, beach and dune scenery, mostly at Migriño Ranch, home of the second, "not so famous" arch.

Camel ride

A unique walk you can only do here: a camel ride along the crystal-clear water and majestic waves of the Pacific. Learn about camel history, anatomy, personality and training. Get up close, pet and feed them, then try typical tacos with authentic Mexican sauces.

Book

Razor desert tour

Explore the Baja California Sur desert on a new Polaris vehicle with an experienced local guide: canyons and mountain trails in a secluded natural area, ideal for couples, families and groups. Helmet and face bandana provided. Includes a visit to the second arch of Cabo San Lucas at Rancho San Cristóbal.

Book

Whale watching

Cabo is one of the best places in the world to watch whales. From December to April, humpback and gray whales visit the warm waters of the Sea of Cortez to mate, give birth and nurse their young. See them by boat, catamaran, kayak or paddleboard.

Book

Glass-bottom boat

A spectacular view of coral reefs, tropical fish and stingrays on the way to the Arch, with views of the beaches and cliffs of Cabo, and the iconic "Window" rock rising from the sea.
